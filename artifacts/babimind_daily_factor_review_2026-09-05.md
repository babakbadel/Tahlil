### New candidate today: `Dual-Rate Pass-Through Asymmetry`
**Definition:** difference in how quickly official-rate changes versus free-market-rate changes pass through to import prices, producer prices, and equity sector earnings.

**Why it is non-redundant:** it is not the FX level or the FX spread; it measures the *speed and asymmetry of transmission* from each pricing regime into the real economy and company margins.

**Expected relationship:** wider spread + faster free-market pass-through -> stronger inflation expectations, higher replacement-cost pressure, and greater divergence between nominal winners and real-economy winners.

**Status:** candidate/watch only; requires aligned official/free-market FX, import-price, sector-margin and market-return series.
